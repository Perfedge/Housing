Know the model before you use it

What does “affordable” mean here?

This lab reserves a chosen share of homes as affordable. It does not calculate rents, income eligibility, or how long restrictions last. A real plan must specify all three. Market-rate homes have no modeled affordability restriction.

Density, height, and land

Density is homes per acre. More density on the same land produces more homes in this model. Height only changes the illustration and a rough capacity warning; it does not change cost or home count. Real buildings also need space for access, setbacks, parking, and other uses.

Follow the money

Homes = floor(acres × homes per acre). Affordable homes = rounded total homes × affordable share. Cost = homes × cost per home + acres × land cost per acre. Funding gap = cost − market revenue − affordable revenue − public subsidy.

Affordable revenue is fixed at an illustrative $250,000 per affordable home. All money values are simplified one-time amounts, not annual rent or household income. Subsidy is treated as available funding, without modeling its source or competing public uses.

What is missing?

No real parcel, zoning approval, displacement estimate, water supply, traffic, climate impact, infrastructure capacity, financing, developer return, or rental cash flow is modeled. Those omissions matter. This is an exploration tool, not a prediction or an adopted plan.

Discuss it with your class

Would your recommendation change if construction costs rose 20%? Who should help decide where homes go? What might renters, workers, neighbors, and future residents value differently? Identify a claim this model cannot support.
